About AS Sidiq

Good work begins with trust.

We bring practical leadership, skilled coordination and straightforward communication to every site we are asked to manage.

Who we are

A builder who stays close to the work.

AS Sidiq Building Contractor is a general contracting business based at 41 Eleweran, Abeokuta, serving clients across Ogun State and nearby areas.

We support property owners, families and businesses through new construction, renovations, site supervision and finishing. Our approach is personal and practical: understand the brief, plan the work properly, coordinate the right hands and pay attention until the final details are complete.

The goal is simple—make the building journey easier to understand and deliver work that earns the client’s confidence.
